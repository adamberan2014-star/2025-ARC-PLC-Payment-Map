How this is calculated

For 2025 only, FSA pays each farm the higher of ARC-CO or PLC for each crop, no matter which program was elected. Amounts are per base acre and include the 85% payment-acre factor. FSA typically begins issuing 2025 payments in October 2026.

ARC-CO: the county's actual yield × the higher of the MYA price or loan rate gives actual revenue. If that falls below the 90% revenue guarantee, the shortfall is paid, capped at 12% of benchmark revenue. Where FSA has already posted a county's rate, the map uses FSA's figure.

PLC: the effective reference price minus the higher of the MYA price or loan rate, times the farm's PLC yield. PLC yields are set per farm, so the map uses 75% of RMA's 2025 expected county yield for the chosen practice (the yield behind ECO and Area Plan; winter wheat where RMA has it) as a stand-in. Enter your own PLC yield on a county for an estimate for your farm.
